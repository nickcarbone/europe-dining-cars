How this was compiled

Every menu comes from the operator’s own website or the caterer it links to. Dish names, printed descriptions and prices are copied as printed, including capitals, diacritics and local price formats such as “8€40”. Allergen codes, calorie counts and footnote markers are left out.

“incl.” marks a dish whose price is part of the fare or the class of travel. The English glosses and notes are editorial.

Menus change with the seasons. Several cards here are summer editions that autumn menus will replace within weeks, and each card gives the date the operator printed.
